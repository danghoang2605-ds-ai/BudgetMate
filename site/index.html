    The real app, tab by tab

    Five tabs, in the order the app shows them. Pick one to see the unedited screenshot and what to look at.

    Net this month
    Income minus spending, with the change from last month in green or red.
    Keypad
    Whole dollars, a 00 key, Expense or Income, a category and a date. Save is the tall gold key.
    Receipt camera
    The camera button opens the scanner. ML Kit reads the receipt on the phone; you confirm the total before it is saved.
    By day
    Entries grouped under their date with each day's net, newest first.
    Filters
    All, Expenses or Income, with counts, for any month you pick.
    Totals
    All-time money in, out and net above the list. Tap an entry to delete it.
    Sentences
    “Lunch 15$” becomes an expense in Food, and the reply says exactly what was saved.
    Quick chips
    Boba $5, Lunch $15, Uber $20 and Salary $200 log with one tap.
    Questions
    Anything that is not a purchase or income gets a short answer instead of an entry.
    Month or year
    Monthly and Annual views, with month and year pickers.
    Charts
    Income against expenses by week, running totals, spending and income by category, and a radar of the main categories.
    Export
    A CSV and a one-page PDF of the selected period.
    Indexes
    S&P 500, Nasdaq and Dow Jones. Sample values unless an Alpha Vantage key is set.
    Top movers
    Six Vietnamese large caps with seven-day trend lines. Tap one for daily candles.
    News
    Finance headlines from GNews on the second tab.

    Three ways to log, pick the fastest

    Keypad
    Amount, category, save. Whole dollars, a 00 key for round numbers, and a date picker for yesterday's lunch.
    Snap the receipt
    Point the camera at a bill. ML Kit reads the text on the phone, the AI finds the total, and you confirm before anything is saved.
    Say it in a sentence
    Type what happened. An AI model turns it into an entry, or answers if you asked a question. The demo shows each step, including what the app does when the model's reply is messy.

    The sentence and receipt features need an AI connection. Builds on GitHub Releases include it when the maintainer has configured the proxy; otherwise the app says the AI is off and the keypad works as usual.

      Your money data stays with you

      No sign-up and no BudgetMate server. The only network calls are the ones you turn on.

      Storage
      Transactions live in an on-device SQLite database (Room). Like most apps, they can be included in Android's own encrypted device backup to your Google account.
      AI
      Optional. Requests go through a small proxy you deploy, so no AI key ships inside the app. Only the sentence or receipt text is sent.
      Network
      HTTPS only. News and stock data load only when you add your own API keys; otherwise the app shows sample data.

      How it's built

      A native Java app by Dang Hoang. The code, tests and CI are open on GitHub.

      Java + MVVM
      Fragments, ViewModel and LiveData over a repository layer.
      Room database
      Versioned schema with explicit migrations, tested on SQLite with Robolectric.
      Retrofit + OkHttp
      News, stocks and the AI chat, with errors shown instead of swallowed.
      ML Kit + CameraX
      On-device text recognition for receipts.
      MPAndroidChart
      Bar, line, pie and radar charts tuned for a dark theme.
      GitHub Actions
      Unit tests, an APK on every push, and these screenshots from an emulator.
